# Northwind Home: brand and listing guide (fictional demo brand)

Version 3. Applies to every marketplace. Owner: brand team.

## Voice

- Warm, plain and practical. Say what the product does for the customer's day.
- Indian English spelling: colour, litre, organise.
- No hype. Never use: best, No.1, number one, premium quality, world class, guaranteed, 100%, sale, offer.

## Facts and claims

- Every fact must come from the supplier documents: size, capacity, weight, material, performance and safety.
- These claims need proof in the supplier documents before we use them. Proof means a test report or declaration reference, such as LT-2291 or SD-118:
  - BPA-free
  - food-grade or food-safe
  - leak-proof
  - insulation hours
  - antibacterial
  - eco-friendly or sustainable
  - certifications
  - warranty
- A manufacturer's own statement is enough for "dishwasher safe", "not microwave safe" and "airtight".
- If a claim has no proof, leave it out. Do not soften it into a near-claim such as "eco-conscious" or "hygienic surface".
- Supplier spreadsheet "Notes" are the supplier's opinion unless they cite a reference. Treat them with care.

## Title (house rule: at most 150 characters)

Formula: Brand + Product type + Key feature, Capacity or size, Material, Colour

Example: Northwind Home Insulated Water Bottle with Leak-Proof Lid, 750 ml, Stainless Steel, Black

- Title Case. No words in capitals except units.
- Only commas, hyphens and brackets as punctuation.

## Bullet points (5 bullets, 40 to 200 characters each)

1. Key performance
2. Material and safety
3. Design and size
4. Care and cleaning
5. What is in the box, or the pack size

Start each bullet with a short benefit phrase and a colon, for example "Cold for 24 Hours: ...". Keep one idea per bullet. Give the number and unit whenever you mention capacity or time.

## Description (150 to 1,000 characters, two short paragraphs)

- Paragraph 1: what it is and who it is for.
- Paragraph 2: materials, care and what is included.

## Search terms (at most 250 bytes)

Lower case, separated by spaces, no commas, no brand names (ours or anyone else's), no claims. Use words shoppers type that are not already in the title: synonyms, uses, occasions.

## Units

- Capacity in ml (1 litre = 1000 ml).
- Weight in grams.
- Dimensions in cm as L x W x H.
